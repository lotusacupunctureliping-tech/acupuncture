What to expect at your first acupuncture appointment

Back to the blog

12 November 2026 · Liping Jiang

If you have not had acupuncture before, it is normal to wonder what will happen when you arrive. This is a simple guide to a first appointment at Lotus Acupuncture, my clinic at 185a Diagonal Road, Warradale, near Glenelg, Brighton, Marion and Morphettville.

You do not need a referral from your doctor to have acupuncture. It is still wise to tell your general practitioner that you are considering it, especially if you are pregnant, take medicines that affect bleeding, or have another health condition your doctor is helping you with.

Before you come in

A little preparation can make the visit more comfortable:

  • Have a light meal so you are not arriving hungry.
  • Wear loose, comfortable clothing. I may need to reach your arms, legs, back or abdomen, depending on the points we use.
  • Bring a list of any medicines or supplements you take, and mention any allergies, implants or blood-borne infections.
  • Allow time so you are not rushing. My initial appointments are 90 minutes.

The consultation

We will start by talking. I will ask about what has brought you in, your medical history, sleep, digestion, energy and daily life. In Chinese medicine this helps me understand the wider picture, not only one symptom.

I may also look at your tongue and feel the pulse at your wrist, and I may gently press certain areas to see if they are tender. There is time for your questions. Please tell me if anything feels unclear or if you would rather not have a particular area examined.

The treatment itself

If we go ahead, I will use fine, sterile, single-use needles at selected points. They stay in place for a time, often around 25 to 35 minutes, while you rest. I will explain what I am doing as we go, and you can ask me to pause or adjust at any point.

Depending on what we have discussed, a visit may also include other traditional Chinese medicine methods I offer, such as cupping, Gua Sha, Chinese massage, electro-acupuncture or moxibustion. We will only use these if they are appropriate for you and you are comfortable with them.

You can read current appointment types and fees on my services and prices page. An initial appointment is $130 for 90 minutes. Follow-up appointments are $100 for 60 minutes.

Afterwards

Some people feel calm or a little tired afterwards. If you feel sleepy, wait until you feel alert before driving. Mild tenderness or a small bruise can happen at a needle site. Drink some water and take the rest of the day at a steady pace if you can.

If you feel unwell after a visit, contact me or your general practitioner. For urgent medical help, call 000.

If you would like to make a first appointment, you can book online through my booking page.

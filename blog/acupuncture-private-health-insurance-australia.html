Is acupuncture covered by private health insurance in Australia?

Back to the blog

12 November 2026 · Liping Jiang

Many people ask whether they can claim acupuncture on their health fund. The short answer is that you often can, but only if your policy includes it, and usually through extras cover rather than hospital cover.

I cannot tell you what your own policy will pay. Cover, rebate amounts, annual limits and waiting periods differ between funds and levels of extras. The only reliable check is to ask your insurer before you come in, or to read your product disclosure statement.

Extras cover, not hospital cover

Acupuncture, when it is covered, is generally listed under extras (sometimes called general treatment). A standard hospital policy does not usually pay for a clinic visit of this kind.

Some extras policies include acupuncture. Others, especially very basic extras that mainly cover dental, may not. A fund may also share your annual acupuncture limit with other natural or complementary therapies. Waiting periods often apply when you first take out or upgrade a policy.

People often ask about well-known funds such as Bupa, Medibank and HCF. Whether acupuncture is included still depends on the specific extras product you hold, so it is best to confirm with your fund.

Who you need to see to claim

Health funds generally only pay a benefit when the practitioner is a recognised provider. For acupuncture this usually means a practitioner who is registered as an acupuncturist with the Chinese Medicine Board of Australia, which is administered by AHPRA.

I am an AHPRA-registered acupuncturist (registration number CMR0002350217). After your visit I will give you a receipt with the details your fund is likely to need. Please check with your insurer about how to claim, including whether they accept electronic claiming at the time of payment.

Does Medicare cover acupuncture?

For a typical visit to a private Chinese medicine clinic, you should not expect a Medicare rebate. The Australian Government’s private health information site states that Medicare does not provide benefits for acupuncture unless it is part of a doctor’s consultation.

Medicare arrangements can be detailed, so if you have a question about a Medicare item, it is best to ask Services Australia or your general practitioner.

Why do I feel flu-like symptoms after moxibustion?

Back to the blog

13 November 2026 · Liping Jiang

It is not uncommon for people to feel a little off after moxibustion — mild body aches, fatigue, a runny nose, chills, or a general “coming down with something” feeling. Many clients are surprised by this, especially if they felt fine before the session. Here is what is usually going on.

The common Traditional Chinese Medicine view

In Traditional Chinese Medicine, moxibustion works by warming the channels, strengthening yang qi, and helping the body move stagnant cold, dampness, or pathogenic factors outward. When this process starts, the body can go through a short “release” or healing reaction.

Typical signs of this include:

  • Mild flu-like body aches
  • Temporary fatigue or drowsiness
  • Sneezing, runny nose, or slight chills
  • Feeling more sensitive to cold for a short time
  • Increased thirst or the need to rest

Practitioners often view these as positive signs that the treatment is mobilising what was stuck. These reactions are usually mild and settle within a few hours to one or two days as the body adjusts.

Other practical reasons

  • Mild immune or circulatory response: The gentle heat stimulates local blood flow and can create a temporary systemic feeling of fatigue or achiness, similar to how some people feel after a strong massage or intense exercise.
  • Timing and coincidence: Sometimes an actual virus was already incubating. The timing simply makes it feel related to the treatment.

Note: Our clinic uses a smokeless moxa machine, which completely eliminates smoke from the burning moxa. This means airway irritation or smoke sensitivity is not a factor in any post-treatment sensations you may notice.

What helps

  • Rest and keep warm after the session.
  • Drink warm water or mild herbal teas (ginger or peppermint are often recommended).
  • Avoid cold drinks, cold food, and strong air-conditioning for the rest of the day.
  • Listen to your body — if you feel tired, sleep.

When to contact your practitioner (or a doctor)

Most mild reactions are short-lived. Get in touch if you experience:

  • Symptoms that worsen or last more than 48 hours
  • High fever, significant shortness of breath, or chest tightness
  • Skin blistering, strong burning pain, or signs of infection at the treatment site
  • Any reaction that feels clearly allergic (severe rash, swelling, breathing difficulty)

Moxibustion is generally well tolerated when performed correctly. Feeling a little “flu-ish” afterward is often just the body responding to the treatment. Most people feel clearer and warmer once the short reaction passes. If you are unsure about anything you are experiencing, a quick message to your practitioner is always welcome.

About

Liping Jiang

Chinese Medicine Practitioner (BHSc Acupuncture, BN, RN)

Liping Jiang completed a Bachelor of Nursing at Shandong University of Traditional Chinese Medicine (2001–2006), and a further Bachelor of Nursing at the University of Adelaide in 2009. She is registered with AHPRA as a Registered Nurse. She subsequently completed a Bachelor of Health Science (Acupuncture) at Endeavour College of Natural Health, Adelaide (2019). She is an AHPRA-registered acupuncturist and a member of the Australian Acupuncture and Chinese Medicine Association (AACMA), and is currently studying a Master of Mental Health Nursing at the University of Newcastle.

She founded Lotus Acupuncture in Warradale in 2024, where she practises Chinese medicine, including acupuncture, electro-acupuncture, cupping, Gua Sha, Chinese massage and moxibustion, with a focus on pain, stress and sleep, women's health, skin, and cosmetic acupuncture.

A holistic approach

Health is viewed as the connection between mind, body, and spirit. Rather than treating symptoms alone, the goal is to address underlying patterns. Personalised care and a clear understanding of traditional Chinese medicine help clients move toward greater vitality and well-being.

Continuing education

Liping regularly takes part in professional development programs and workshops to stay current with advances in acupuncture and holistic medicine. This ongoing learning supports the quality of care offered at the practice.

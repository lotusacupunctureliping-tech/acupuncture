About

Liping Jiang

Chinese Medicine Practitioner (BHSc Acupuncture, BN, RN)

Liping Jiang is a registered nurse and acupuncturist based in Adelaide. She began her healthcare career with a Bachelor of Nursing from Shandong University of Traditional Chinese Medicine (2001–2006), followed by a further Bachelor of Nursing from the University of Adelaide in 2009. She is registered with AHPRA as a Registered Nurse.

She later completed a Bachelor of Health Science (Acupuncture) at Endeavour College of Natural Health in Adelaide (2019) and is an AHPRA-registered acupuncturist and a member of the Australian Acupuncture and Chinese Medicine Association (AACMA). She is currently undertaking a Master of Mental Health Nursing at the University of Newcastle.

In 2024 she founded Lotus Acupuncture in Warradale. There she offers traditional Chinese medicine treatments including acupuncture, electro-acupuncture, cupping, Gua Sha, Chinese massage and moxibustion. Her practice focuses on pain management, stress and sleep, women's health, skin concerns, and cosmetic acupuncture.

A holistic approach

Liping takes a holistic approach, looking at the connection between mind, body and spirit rather than symptoms alone. She continues to engage in professional development to keep her skills current and provide thoughtful, personalised care.

I’m committed to proper licensing, thorough consultations, clear communication, and safe practices.

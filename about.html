About Lotus Acupuncture

Lotus Acupuncture was established by Dr. Liping Jiang and is based in Glenelg. It specialises in cosmetic acupuncture, women's health, men's health, stress and anxiety, pulmonary disorders, degenerative issues, chronic pain and skin issues, using acupuncture, cupping, Gua Sha, Chinese massage, electronic acupuncture and moxibustion.

I am passionate about helping my clients achieve holistic well-being and optimal health.

Our holistic approach

We believe in the interconnectedness of mind, body and spirit. Our services are designed to address the root causes of health issues. Through personalised treatments and a deep understanding of traditional Chinese medicine, we aim to help you achieve balance, vitality and overall well-being.

Ongoing learning

I am committed to continuous learning and staying up to date with advances in holistic medicine and acupuncture techniques. I regularly take part in professional development programs and workshops to improve my skills and the care I give.
